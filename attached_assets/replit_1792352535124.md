# Sanad - سند

## Problem
Many customers in Kuwait want to tip co-op workers to show appreciation for their service but do not carry cash. As a result, customers may miss the opportunity to reward workers, and workers have limited convenient ways to receive small tips digitally.

## User
Primary users: Co-op workers who want to receive digital tips.

Secondary users: Customers who want to appreciate workers by leaving tips.

Admin: A platform administrator who manages worker profiles and monitors platform activity.

## Solution
Sanad is a digital tipping platform that allows customers to scan a worker's unique QR code, view their profile, choose a tip amount, and complete a simulated digital tip. Workers can access a dashboard to view their received tips and transaction history.

## Differentiator
Sanad focuses specifically on making tipping accessible in Kuwait's co-ops, where customers may not have cash available. Instead of requiring cash, the platform connects customers directly to workers through unique QR codes. Its simple, worker-focused design makes showing appreciation quick, convenient, and transparent.

## MVP features (build only these)
- User registration and login for customers and workers.
- Worker profiles displaying a worker's name, workplace, and unique QR code.
- QR code tipping allowing customers to scan a worker's code and open their profile.
- Simulated digital payments allowing customers to select a tip amount and complete a demo transaction without real money.
- Worker dashboard displaying total tips received and recent transactions.
- Transaction history allowing workers to review individual tips and their amounts.
- Admin dashboard allowing the administrator to manage worker profiles and review demo transactions.
- Digital tip confirmation showing customers a success message and receipt after a simulated tip.

## Vision (do not build yet)
In the future, Sanad aims to become a trusted digital tipping platform used across co-ops and other service businesses in Kuwait. Potential additions include approved real-money payments, customer reward points, multilingual support, partnerships with co-ops, and expanded transaction reporting. The long-term vision is to make digital appreciation simple, accessible, and convenient for both customers and workers.
