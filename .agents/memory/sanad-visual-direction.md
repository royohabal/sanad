---
name: SANAD visual direction
description: Product constraint for SANAD screen hierarchy, copy density, and preserving functional flows.
---

Keep the SANAD interface concise and easy to understand at a glance. Use short headings and descriptions, visual summaries, and one clear primary action per screen while retaining the existing warm brand palette and responsive behavior.

**Why:** The instructor's feedback was that the website felt crowded with words. The redesign should clarify the experience without removing the existing working features.

**How to apply:** When changing SANAD screens, reduce repeated explanations and secondary labels, but preserve routes, data, authentication, QR and wallet actions, and truthful distinctions between Stripe test and simulated checkout.
